Transcribe turns your recordings into text right away. And with the new AI Notes feature, it turns that text into a summary and a list of action items.

■ AI Notes
Create a summary and action items from your transcript with a single tap.
- AI Notes are generated entirely on your device. Your audio and transcript are not sent to a server to be summarized
- No account required. No extra charge
* AI Notes require a device that supports Apple Intelligence. On unsupported devices, the app tells you so instead of failing silently

■ Features
- Real-time transcription: see the text appear while you record
- Transcribe after recording: convert long recordings with a progress indicator, and pause or resume anytime
- AI Notes: pull out a summary and action items automatically
- Import files: transcribe audio files, video files, or a URL from your device
- Append recordings: record more later and merge it into a single file
- Copy and share: copy the transcript with one tap, or share the text and the audio file together

■ Languages
Transcription currently supports Japanese.

■ Who it's for
- Anyone who needs a record of meetings and calls
- Journalists and researchers turning interviews into text
- Students reviewing lectures and seminars
- Anyone who prefers to capture ideas by voice

■ Good to know
- Your recordings are stored on your device
- Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition
- The app shows ads
